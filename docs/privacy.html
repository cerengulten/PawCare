Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

Welcome to Bisco ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application and related services.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: When you register an account or sign in with Google/Apple/Email, we collect your email address, name, and profile authentication tokens.
  • Pet Profile Information: Information about your dogs such as name, breed, date of birth, weight, gender, sterilization status, and photos you choose to upload.
  • Health & Activity Records: Data you input into the app, including meals, water logs, poop and health symptom records, vaccine certificates, parasite treatments, heat cycles, and veterinary visits.
  • Location Data: When using the Vet Finder feature, we may access your approximate or precise location solely to display nearby veterinary clinics and calculate distances on the map. We do not store or track your continuous location history.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, maintain, and personalize your pet care journal experience.
  • To sync your pet's records seamlessly across your logged-in devices via secure cloud database (Supabase).
  • To send you push notifications and timely reminders for upcoming vaccines, parasite treatments, or medications (you can disable these at any time).
  • To provide AI pet-care conversational assistance tailored to your pet's profile.

3. How We Share and Protect Your Data

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Your data is stored securely using industry-standard encryption protocols on Supabase infrastructure with Row Level Security (RLS) policies ensuring only you have access to your pet's logs.

4. Account and Data Deletion

You have full ownership and control over your data. You may delete your account and all associated pet records, images, and history at any time directly within the app (Profile → Delete Account). Once confirmed, all your personal data is permanently deleted from our servers.

5. Third-Party Services

We utilize trusted third-party providers for critical functions:

  • Supabase: Cloud database, authentication, and encrypted data storage.
  • Google Identity Services: Secure single-sign-on (OAuth).
  • Expo / EAS: Mobile application framework and update delivery.
  • OpenStreetMap / Overpass: Public map and veterinary clinic directory search.

6. Children's Privacy

Bisco is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13.
